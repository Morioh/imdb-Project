IMDb Schema: ER Diagram

Eleven tables. title and person are the two central entities. Every foreign key sits on the "many" side and points to the "one" side's primary key.

How to read it

SymbolMeaning
Crow's foot: the "many" side
Double bar: the "one" side
PKPrimary key. Two PK columns in one table form a composite key.
FKForeign key, pointing to the PK of the table the line connects to

Notes

  • title_rating is 1:1 with title. Its PK is also its FK.
  • title_episode links to title twice: tconst (the episode itself, 1:1) and parentTconst (the series, one series to many episodes).
  • title_principal, title_director, title_writer, and person_known_for are bridge tables between title and person. In title_principal, nconst is an FK but not part of the PK, because one person can hold more than one role in the same title.
  • In this design, genre is stored as text in title_genre. There is no separate genre table.
